
# Cash Flow and Debt Forecast

## Workbook Contents
This workbook contains the projected Cash Flow and Debt schedules for FY2026E to FY2030E. It brings together outputs from the Profit and Loss forecast with cash movement and debt-related calculations.

## Financial Calculations
The schedules calculate cash flow movements and closing cash balances, along with debt repayments and closing debt balances. Interest expense is incorporated into the debt and financial forecast, linking financing costs with projected results.

## Formulas and Modelling Techniques
- Linked cell references to bring relevant forecast figures into the cash flow and debt schedules.
- Roll-forward calculations connecting opening balances, period movements and closing balances.
- Debt repayment and closing debt calculations across the forecast period.
- Interest calculations linked to the debt schedule and incorporated into the financial forecast.

## Final Output
The workbook provides projected closing cash and closing debt for each forecast year. It demonstrates how forecast earnings, cash movements and financing activities connect across an integrated financial model.

*This is an educational financial-modelling project. Forecast figures are estimates based on model assumptions, not actual company results.*
